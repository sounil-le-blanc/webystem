The Artisan Web Manifesto

← Back to site

I. The Observation

The web has become an industry. Code factories. Endless subscriptions. Websites that weigh 5 MB to display three paragraphs and an image.

You are promised turnkey solutions. You are locked into tools you do not master. You are billed every month for a service that never belongs to you.

They industrialized the web. They made it obese, slow, dependent.

We say: enough.

II. The Return to Craft

We believe a website is a crafted object. Like a table. Like a storefront. Like a roped access tool.

A craftsperson chooses their materials. Assembles them with care. Signs their work. Guarantees its longevity.

We do the same. Our materials are HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The three languages of the web for 30 years. No framework that expires in 18 months. No dependency that breaks at the next update. Raw, readable, durable code.

A crafted website weighs 50 KB. It loads in a fraction of a second. It needs no maintenance. It will still be online in 10 years.

III. Sovereignty

A craftsperson does not keep the keys to your house. They hand them back when the work is done.

We apply this principle to the web. The domain name is in your name. The source code is delivered to you. Emails, database — everything is on your own accounts. You are home.

If you want to switch providers, you take your things and leave. In 5 minutes. Owing nothing.

We do not sell prisons. We build houses.

IV. The Low-Tech Method

We use AI as a tool, not as an autopilot. It accelerates the work, but the craftsperson decides, reviews, and assembles.

We repurpose big tech services to offer world-class infrastructure at minimal cost. Vercel, Supabase, Cloudflare, Resend, Stripe. The best tools in the world, used sparingly, without toxic dependency.

Everything else, we do ourselves. The code. The design. The logic. The interface. The experience.

No bloat. No turnkey digital solution that locks you in. Just simple, robust, effective tools.

V. Transmission

Craft knowledge is shared, not patented.

Our code is open-source. Our method is documented. We teach those who want to learn. We welcome those who want to practice.

We are building a guild. A network of web artisans who share the same principles: simplicity, durability, sovereignty.

Everyone is free. Everyone is supported. Everyone enriches the ecosystem.

VI. The Call

This manifesto is not a sales pitch. It is an invitation.

To craftspeople, merchants, and freelancers who are tired of solutions that complicate everything. To developers who feel the industry has gone too far. To the curious who want to understand how to do more with less.

Join us. Learn the method. Build your tools. Take back control.

The artisan web is being reborn. And this time, it will not disappear.

— Webystem. OldStack. The web of yesterday, with the tools of today.
